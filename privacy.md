# 德州有数 · Riverwise 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 19 日。适用于德州有数 iPhone App。

## 牌局数据

App 无需注册账号。你输入的手牌、公牌、玩家设置和动作记录用于在本机计算概率和保存牌局。开发者不会收到这些记录。App 不接入第三方广告或分析 SDK。

你可以在 App 的「历史」中删除单条记录，或通过 iOS 的「删除 App」移除设备中的应用数据。系统备份由你的 Apple 账户设置管理。

## 订阅与付款

进阶模式的订阅和付款由 Apple App Store 处理。App 使用 Apple 提供的订阅状态确认进阶模式是否可用。开发者不会收到你的银行卡信息或 Apple 账户密码。你可以在 iPhone 的「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中管理或取消订阅。

[Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 联系方式

如果你通过邮件联系支持，开发者会使用你的邮箱和来信内容处理请求。请勿发送密码或付款凭据。隐私问题可联系 [德州有数支持](mailto:dantesx2012@gmail.com)。

# Riverwise Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026. This policy applies to Riverwise for iPhone.

## Hand history

Riverwise does not require an account. Hole cards, community cards, player settings, and recorded actions are processed and saved on your device to calculate odds and maintain hand history. The developer does not receive these records. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

You can delete individual hands in History or use Delete App in iOS to remove the app’s data from your device. Your Apple Account settings control system backups.

## Subscriptions and payments

Apple handles subscriptions and payments through the App Store. Riverwise uses the subscription status provided by Apple to determine access to Advanced mode. The developer does not receive your card details or Apple Account password. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

[Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## Contact

If you email support, the developer uses your email address and message to respond. Please avoid sending passwords or payment credentials. For privacy questions, contact [Riverwise Support](mailto:dantesx2012@gmail.com).
