# 德州有数 · Riverwise

德州有数是一款 iPhone 上的德州扑克概率计算与牌局复盘工具。录入手牌和公牌即可查看胜率、平分概率、摊牌权益和最终牌型分布。

- **简单模式免费。** 可以随时计算牌局概率。试用结束后，简单模式仍可使用。
- **进阶模式通过年订阅解锁。** 进阶模式支持设置玩家风格、逐街记录动作和估算对手范围。符合条件的用户可免费试用 3 天。中国大陆年费 ¥69，美国年费 US$9.90，其他地区价格及试用资格以 Apple 的购买确认页面为准。
- 试用结束后，Apple 将按年费扣款，订阅按年自动续订，除非你取消。你可在 iPhone 的「设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅」中管理或取消订阅。

Riverwise is a Texas Hold’em odds calculator and hand review tool for iPhone.

- **Simple mode is free** and stays available after the trial ends.
- **Advanced mode requires an annual subscription.** It includes player styles, street-by-street action tracking, and opponent range estimates. Eligible users can try it free for 3 days. The annual price is ¥69 in mainland China and US$9.90 in the United States. Check Apple's purchase sheet for your local price and trial eligibility.
- After the trial, Apple charges the annual price, and the subscription renews annually unless canceled. You can manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings under your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

For support, email [Riverwise Support](mailto:dantesx2012@gmail.com) with your iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue.
